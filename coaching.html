What it is

I'm a certified personal trainer. What you get is a training program built for the week you actually have, habit coaching, food structure (recipes, portions and a shape for the day, not a diet), and a structure for your sleep, steps and recovery.

The method is short: assess, show you the number, build the program, retest in eight weeks.

What it is not

I am not your doctor and I am not a diet professional. I do not read blood work, I do not prescribe diets or doses, I do not tell you which supplements to take, and I do not diagnose anything. Doctor questions go to the doctor. If you are on medication, your doctor is part of the conversation before any food change.

Who it is for

You have read the free protocol and want it built around your real schedule, your condo gym or no gym at all, and your own starting numbers. You want to be measured, not motivated.

If what you want is a diet prescribed, a lab result interpreted or a diagnosis, that is your doctor, not me.

Where

Toronto. Your condo gym, your home, or a park. I do not coach on a commercial gym floor. Online coaching is not open yet.

What the first session looks like

Sixty minutes. We go through your intake form together. I watch you move through the main patterns (a squat, a hinge, a push, a pull, a carry) and take your starting numbers. You leave with your first week written down. We retest in eight weeks.

$50 an hour, in person.
