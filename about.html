About Daniel Beltran, trainer in Toronto

I'm Daniel Beltran. I live in Toronto, Canada. I was born in Colombia and came here in January 2022.

I'm a certified personal trainer. I work at a community charity fitness centre, and I'm starting a private coaching practice in Toronto: in homes, condo gyms and parks, and online later.

I'm finishing my high-school credits in math and English as an adult student, on the way to a Kinesiology degree in Toronto.

Spanish is my first language and English my second. This site is English first; the Spanish pages follow once the English ones are solid.

I think health is body and mind at the same time, and that what every human has to do for both can be written down plainly.

I think this knowledge should be free for everybody, with the sources shown. The lookup behind these pages was built from 110 sources and 2,525 graded claims, and every line on the protocol page carries its grade.

I learn from lived experience first and theory second, and I hold to one rule: no one is above anyone.

I run a 5K in 26:53 and I cook my own food from whole ingredients.

I post on Instagram.
