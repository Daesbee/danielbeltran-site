Verum app: privacy policy

Verum is a personal app I built for my own training and coaching. Right now it has one user: me. Last updated 26 October 2026.

What it reads from Google

Only if you sign in with Google and press Allow, Verum reads your Google Health data, read-only:

  • Sleep.
  • Health metrics and measurements: heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, weight, body fat, breathing rate and blood oxygen.
  • Activity and fitness: workouts and steps.

It cannot write, change or delete anything in your Google account.

What happens to it

  • It is saved on the computer that runs Verum, and used only to show you your own numbers and plan your training.
  • Verum's assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) reads these numbers inside your own conversations with it, to answer you. That is the only place the data goes.
  • It is never sold, never used for ads, and never shared with anyone else.
  • Verum's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping and deleting

  • You can remove Verum's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask me and I delete everything Verum saved about you.
